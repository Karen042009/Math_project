Why this estimates π

Each point lands somewhere in the square at random. The quarter circle covers exactly π / 4 of the square's area, so the fraction of points inside it approaches π / 4. Multiply that fraction by four: π ≈ 4 × (inside ÷ total). More points usually bring the estimate closer.
